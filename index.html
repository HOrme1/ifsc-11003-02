Banana

Bananas are a type of fruit that are rich in nutrients and provide numerous health benefits. They are a good source of vitamins, minerals, and fiber.

  • Rich in potassium
  • Good source of vitamin C
  • Contains dietary fiber
  • Supports heart health

Bananas can be eaten on their own, added to smoothies, or used in baking recipes. They are a versatile and delicious fruit that can be enjoyed in many ways.
